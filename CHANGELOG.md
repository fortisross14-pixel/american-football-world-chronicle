# Changelog

## v1.1 — Dynasties & Legacy

The universe now remembers. Champions chase history, stars hit the market, and the
offseason's biggest moves are revealed card by card.

**New**
- **Legend & Hall of Fame cards.** Legend and Generational retirements (top 3) and every
  Hall of Fame inductee get a tribute card: portrait, career span, every team they played
  for, career stats and honors.
- **Trade reveals.** The top trades flip like free agency: traded by → player → acquired
  by, with what was paid.
- **Honors on cards.** Free-agency and trade cards show the player's current rating and
  career honors (MVPs, DPOYs, All-Pros, Pro Bowls, rings).
- **Skip.** Next to 1× / 2× during a watched game: jump straight to the result.
- **Pre-game dashboard.** Before every watched game: win chances (from the same
  expected margin the engine plays with) and a colour-coded comparison of overall,
  passing, rushing, defense and K/P + staff, green strong to red weak, with arrows on
  each edge.
- **Post-game result.** After each watched game, an animated card announces what it
  means ("…are going to the Super Bowl for the first time ever!", upsets, comebacks,
  overtime, droughts), with the game MVP and key team stats. Review the game or
  continue to the next one.
- **Watch from the Divisional round.** The finale show can now start with the four
  NFL divisional games (or the four CFP quarterfinals).
- **Rarity frames.** Every portrait is framed in its rarity colour (Epic, Legend and
  Generational glow); matchup previews show rarity tags.
- **Big-play bubbles.** While you watch a game (Game of the Week replay or the finale
  show), every touchdown, sack, interception and safety pops a bubble with the player's
  face and name for about three seconds.
- **Card reveals.** The top free-agent signings, top transfers and the top 3 draft picks
  flip in three beats: where he came from → who he is → where he went. Legend and
  Generational reveals land with a gold pop.
- **Dynasties.** Title headlines know history: back-to-back, THREE-PEAT, 4 titles in
  6 years, droughts ended, first titles. New *History → Dynasties* tab names each era,
  and team pages hang a banner for every title.
- **Dynasty ceiling.** No team can win four straight, five in six years or six in ten.
  A team at the ceiling can still reach the title game, and gets denied there.
- **Defending champions.** "Can they repeat?" stories before the season, *Dethroned*
  headlines when they fall, and *Champions raided* when rivals poach their coaches
  and players.
- **Preseason power rankings & title odds** on Home, judged at season end: long-shot
  champions, the surprise team and the bust of the year.
- **Free agency board.** Before the market opens: the top 10 pending free agents, their
  odds of staying and three teams to watch.
- **Contract years & ring chasers.** Stars in the last year of their deal get stories
  and a player-page flag; veterans over 30 chase contenders and get a payoff headline if
  they win it all.
- **Offseason report cards.** Every team gets an A+ to F for how much stronger or weaker
  it got.
- **Playoff field set.** End-of-season headline with the No. 1 seeds, the last team in
  and the heartbreak team that just missed.
- Title celebrations show the dynasty badge (BACK-TO-BACK, THREE-PEAT, DROUGHT OVER…).

**Changes**
- The season ends on the Super Bowl: the National Championship is played (and celebrated)
  first.
- Awards night reveals four headline awards, building up: Heisman, Offensive Player of
  the Year, Defensive Player of the Year, NFL MVP. The full list follows.
- Draft night counts down: pick 3, pick 2, then No. 1.
- Stars re-sign less automatically, so the market has real names:
  Generational 94% → 80%, Legend 90% → 74%, Epic 84% → 70%. Ambitious players on
  losing teams and veterans on non-contenders are more likely to leave.
- About 5 marquee signings per offseason (was about 2), with 1–4 Epic-or-better
  free agents per year (was 0).
- Big individual games now say why they matter (a star living up to the billing, a
  rookie, a contract year), and the same player can't lead the headlines every week.

**Quality of life**
- *Skip to next reveal* in the offseason: 3 taps instead of 8, stopping at every card
  reveal.
- Tab bars no longer push pages wider than a phone screen (Market page fixed).
- Long saves stay smaller and faster: minor players retired more than 5 years ago whom
  no record, award, title or story mentions are archived out of the save.
- Leaner history: quarterback rivalries keep totals (record, playoff meetings, biggest
  stage, last meeting); full game-by-game logs stay on team-vs-team rivalries. Past
  seasons keep full stat lines only for the top 20 in each stat category; everyone
  else keeps year, team, rating, games and grade, and career totals stay exact.

**Fixes**
- When both teams wear similar colours, the away side switches to white on the
  scoreboard, field, win-probability and drive charts.
- Plural team names read correctly ("the Bengals acquire", "the Titans defeat").
